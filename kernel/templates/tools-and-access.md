## Notes

- A listed tool is company-supported for the stated use; it does not mean every user is connected.
- Current-user access and permission are checked live when the work needs them.
- A scheduled Routine declares the identity available to its unattended run and that identity's human owner. Do not assume the configuring user's current connection will be present.
- Store only an understandable identity label and business owner here. Credentials and private host connection identifiers stay in the host or secrets manager.
- Tools support Roles. They are not business Systems, Roles or accountable owners.
